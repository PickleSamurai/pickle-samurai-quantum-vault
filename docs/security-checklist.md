# Web3 Security Checklist

## Wallet Safety

- Never share your seed phrase or private key.
- Use a hardware wallet for larger holdings.
- Do not keep all assets in one wallet.
- Use a fresh wallet for long-term storage.
- Review wallet activity regularly.

## Token Approval Safety

- Be careful with unlimited token approvals.
- Revoke old or suspicious approvals.
- Avoid connecting your wallet to unknown websites.
- Double-check URLs before signing transactions.
- Never sign messages you do not understand.

## Smart-Contract Safety

- Check if the contract is verified.
- Review ownership and admin controls.
- Be cautious with new or unknown tokens.
- Watch for unrealistic promises or guaranteed returns.
- Check liquidity, holder activity, and project transparency.

## Quantum-Readiness Awareness

- Understand that future quantum computing may affect older cryptographic systems.
- Avoid unnecessary wallet exposure.
- Follow blockchain and post-quantum security research.
- Use strong security habits today while future standards develop.
