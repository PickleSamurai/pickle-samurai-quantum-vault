# Quantum Risk Framework

## Purpose

This document outlines a beginner-friendly framework for explaining future quantum-related security exposure in Web3 wallets.

The goal is not to claim that a wallet is “quantum-proof.” The goal is to help users understand risk, improve wallet safety habits, and prepare for future post-quantum blockchain security developments.

## Risk Factors

A wallet may have higher future exposure if:

- It has a large amount of public on-chain activity.
- It has interacted with many unknown contracts.
- It has held significant value for long periods.
- It has old or risky token approval history.
- It does not use strong security practices.
- It frequently signs messages on unknown websites.
- It keeps all assets in one wallet instead of separating risk.

## Risk Levels

### Low Risk

The wallet has limited exposure, few risky interactions, and follows basic security practices.

### Medium Risk

The wallet has moderate public activity, some contract interactions, or approval history that should be reviewed.

### High Risk

The wallet has high-value activity, many risky interactions, suspicious approvals, or poor security practices.

## Recommended User Actions

Users can reduce risk by:

- Using a hardware wallet for larger holdings.
- Moving long-term assets to a fresh wallet.
- Revoking old or suspicious approvals.
- Avoiding unknown websites and suspicious wallet connection requests.
- Separating high-value assets from everyday trading wallets.
- Following trusted blockchain and post-quantum security research.

## Educational Note

This framework is for education, research, and open-source security awareness. It does not provide financial advice, investment advice, legal advice, or guaranteed protection against current or future threats.
